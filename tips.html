🌿 Eco-Friendly Tips

Small changes in your daily life can make a big difference to the environment. Follow these simple tips to live more sustainably.

🌱 1. Save Electricity

Turn off lights, fans, and appliances when they are not in use.

💧 2. Conserve Water

Close taps properly and avoid wasting water while brushing or washing.

♻️ 3. Recycle Waste

Separate recyclable and non-recyclable waste before disposal.

🛍️ 4. Use Reusable Bags

Carry cloth or reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags.

🚲 5. Walk or Cycle

Choose walking or cycling for short distances to reduce pollution.

🌳 6. Plant Trees

Plant and care for trees to improve air quality and reduce carbon dioxide.

🍽️ 7. Reduce Food Waste

Take only the food you need and avoid wasting meals.

🔋 8. Save Energy

Use LED bulbs and switch off electrical devices when not needed.

📄 9. Go Paperless

Use digital notes, bills, and documents whenever possible.

🚌 10. Use Public Transport

Travel by bus, train, or metro to reduce traffic and pollution.

🌍 Every Small Action Counts!

Together, we can create a cleaner, greener, and healthier future.
